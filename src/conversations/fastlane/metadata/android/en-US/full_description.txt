Zungvi Messenger is a privacy-friendly messaging app for staying connected with friends, family, teams, and communities without surrendering your data to large corporations.

Use Zungvi Messenger with your Zungvi account or another compatible XMPP account.

Zungvi Messenger supports secure chat, group conversations, media sharing, and modern XMPP features while keeping the app simple to use.

The app is free and open-source software.

Learn more at https://zungvi.com/help

This app is based on Snikket and the XMPP client Conversations.
